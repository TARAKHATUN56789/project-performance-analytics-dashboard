**🚀 Project: Project Performance Analytics Dashboard**

Most portfolio projects show either data analysis *or* project management skills. I wanted to show both — because that's what actually happens on real teams.

**The problem:** A software company running 300+ projects across 7 departments has no single view of what's on track, what's delayed, where budget is leaking, and what needs urgent attention.

**What I built:**
📊 Cleaned and merged two relational datasets (300 projects, 9,650+ tasks) in Python/Pandas
🧮 Wrote and tested 10 SQL queries answering real business questions
📈 Built 13 analytical charts + an interactive Excel dashboard (live formulas, zero errors)
📱 Designed a multi-page Power BI dashboard with KPI cards, slicers, and drill-through
📝 Managed the project itself like a PM — charter, risk log, status updates, retrospective

**What I found:**
→ 36% of projects missed their deadline (avg. 18 days late)
→ 51% of projects ran over budget
→ A small set of teams and managers consistently outperform the rest — their practices are worth replicating company-wide

**Why this project:** I'm applying to the Google Apprenticeship Program in Bangalore, and I wanted a project that reflects how the role actually works — using data to make a business decision, not just making a chart.
